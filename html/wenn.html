Financial infrastructure + AI risk engine.

Don't build another wallet UI. Build the part that makes money movement correct, traceable and safe: ledger, retries, refunds, reconciliation and fraud signals.

One strict rule: AI can flag fraud. It cannot decide where the money went. Ledger correctness stays deterministic.

The actual takeaway

Don't build more.
Build deeper.

One deeply built project can give you more to talk about in an interview than ten clones. Show the decisions, failures, benchmarks and tradeoffs, not just screenshots.

Explain data flow and failure paths.
